Whale tape

    How to read it

    Every bubble is one aggressive order (fills from the same taker order at the same millisecond are merged), placed at its average fill price. Bubble area scales with dollar value. Green = buyer hit the ask, red = seller hit the bid.

    Long idea: clusters of big buy bubbles mark where size is accumulating. A pullback into an untouched buy shelf is a level where buyers already stepped in.

    Short idea: clusters of big sell bubbles above price mark distribution. A rally back into an untouched sell shelf is a level sellers defended.

    A shelf stops extending once a candle closes through it. Bubbles build up from the moment the page is open (plus a short backfill), and are kept on this device between visits.
